End-to-End Order Workflow

How an order moves from restaurant request to delivery confirmation and automated commission accounting.

1

Order Submission

Restaurant browses wholesale listings, specifies quantity, delivery date, and address, then submits an order request.

2

Supplier Review

Farmer reviews the order specs, inventory availability, and accepts or rejects the incoming request with notes.

3

Dispatch & Logistics

Supplier marks order as dispatched, attaching transit details (driver contact, vehicle plate, cold-chain status).

4

Delivery Confirmation

Restaurant kitchen inspects goods upon arrival and confirms receipt, or files a dispute if specifications do not match.

5

Commission Calculation

System automatically calculates the platform's brokerage fee and records net supplier payout in Nigerian Naira (₦).
